# Code of conduct

We welcome participation regardless of background, identity or experience.

Be respectful, give constructive technical feedback, credit others' work, accept
corrections and respect privacy. Harassment, threats, sexualized conduct, personal
attacks, discriminatory language, doxxing and continued unwanted contact are not
acceptable in project spaces or when representing the project.

Report concerns privately to **alansalazarfg2@gmail.com**. Alan Salazar
([@alanslzrr](https://github.com/alanslzrr)) maintains this project and handles
reports. Include relevant context without publishing private information.

The maintainer may clarify expectations, remove inappropriate content, warn a
participant, temporarily restrict participation or ban repeated/severe misconduct.
Responses depend on severity and available evidence. Reports will be handled as
privately as practical; confidentiality cannot be guaranteed when safety or legal
obligations require disclosure. If you believe a decision was mistaken, reply to
the private contact with additional context and request reconsideration.

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, discussions and other project spaces.
It does not create a commercial support commitment.
